## 💡 Study Tips

1. **Subnetting is king** — Practice IPv4/IPv6 subnetting every single day during Phase 1 & 2. Use subnettingpractice.com.
2. **Use Packet Tracer** — Cisco Packet Tracer (free) or Cisco Modeling Labs for hands-on labs.
3. **Take notes by hand** — Studies show handwritten notes improve retention by 30%+.
4. **Don't skip quizzes** — They reinforce learning and identify gaps immediately.
5. **Watch at 1.25x–1.5x speed** — Kevin Wallace speaks clearly; you can speed up if comfortable.
6. **Schedule exam early** — Book your CCNA exam at the start of November to create accountability.
7. **Rest days matter** — Use 1 day/week for review, not new content. Burnout is the #1 reason people quit.
8. **Join a study group** — Reddit r/ccna, r/ccnp, or Discord communities for motivation.
